Most businesses don't notice a security problem until it's already a problem.

Modern organizations depend on websites, Wi-Fi, computers, accounts and digital data every day. A single weak password, vulnerable website, unsafe network or phishing mistake can expose important information. Kavach helps organizations identify security weaknesses before they become expensive problems.

01

Website Exposure

Outdated plugins, exposed admin paths or missing security headers leave digital storefronts vulnerable.

02

Unsafe Wi-Fi & Networks

Default router credentials, shared staff/guest passwords or flat office networks invite unauthorized access.

03

Data & Account Risks

Unmanaged admin accounts, unencrypted sensitive files and missing MFA expose organizational records.

04

Phishing & Human Error

Deceptive emails, fake login portals and social engineering bypass perimeter firewalls through people.
